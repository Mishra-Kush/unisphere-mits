About UniSphere

College Activity and Club Information System

UniSphere brings the clubs, events, calendar and announcements of MITS-DU, Gwalior together in one place, so students can find what is happening and register in one click.

How it is built

This is a first-year college microproject: Design and Development of Integrated College Activity and Club Information System. The backend is written in C and stores data in SQLite. The pages use plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Good to know

Student accounts require a college email ending in @mitsgwl.ac.in. The domain is checked, but email ownership is not verified yet. Club pages link to each club's own Instagram page; UniSphere does not copy or sync anything from Instagram. Advisor names, student leads and recruitment status show "Not yet provided" or "To be announced" until an administrator fills them in.
